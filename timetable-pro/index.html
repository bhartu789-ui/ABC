What does conflict-free mean?

A teacher cannot be placed in two classes in the same period. The tool also checks weekly subject counts, available periods and locked lessons. A timetable is marked complete only if every required lesson is placed. For unusually difficult combinations, the generator may ask you to change allocations or unlock periods.
